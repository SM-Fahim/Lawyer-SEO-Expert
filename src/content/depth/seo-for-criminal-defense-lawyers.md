## Which charge and county pages should a criminal defense firm build?

A criminal defense firm should build a page for each charge it wants more of and one for each court where it appears. A charge-and-county page earns its place only where that pairing is a real share of the work.

Each row below is a page that answers one kind of search, so the call lands on a page built for it. Bracketed words stand for your own charge, county, district or state.

| Page | The search it answers | Build it when |
|---|---|---|
| Charge page | "[charge] lawyer [state]" | You want more of that charge |
| Court or county page | "criminal defense lawyer [county]" | You appear in that court regularly |
| Charge in one county | "[charge] lawyer [county]" | That charge is a real share of your work there |
| First appearance page | "what happens at a first appearance in [county]" | Your county runs its own steps |
| Family page | "[relative] arrested, what do I do" | Families make a share of your first calls |
| Federal page | "federal [charge] lawyer [district]" | You practice in that federal district |
| Juvenile page | "juvenile lawyer [county]" | You take juvenile cases |
| Record clearing page | "[state] expungement lawyer" | You file record-clearing petitions |

How to write each of these, section by section, is in our [criminal defense SEO guide](/criminal-defense-seo-guide/). This page covers which ones to build first and how the work runs.

### What turns a county page into a doorway?

A county page turns into a doorway when nothing on it belongs to that county. Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) treat region pages built only to push visitors toward one destination as doorway abuse.

Our test is a swap. Put a neighboring county's name in place of this one. If every sentence still reads as true, the page is a doorway risk; if the swap makes it wrong, the page is real. Real county pages are how one office reaches past its own county.

## How does one defense office rank in more than one county?

Distance is one of the three things Google weighs for Map results, beside relevance and prominence, so a defense office competes hardest near its own address ([how local ranking works](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)). We build reach into other counties through the website.

[slot proof]

Google's [guidelines for business profiles](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) set the limits on stretching a listing:

[checklist]
- **Service area.** It should stay within about 2 hours' driving time of the office, though Google allows more for some businesses.
- **Mail drops.** A rented address the firm doesn't work from isn't eligible, and a virtual office counts only if it is staffed in business hours.
- **Name.** The listing carries the firm's real name, with no county or charge added.
- **A real second office.** A staffed office in another county, open in its stated hours, is a location of its own.

Before we plan county pages, we record who holds the Map pack and the first results for your top charges in each county. Those can be local firms, firms from the next county, or directories. That record decides which county gets a page first.

Reviews feed prominence, and defense clients may not want their name next to a case in public, so reviews come slowly. Google bars asking only the clients you expect to be happy ([review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114)). Our routine asks every client at the close of the case and accepts a slower count.

## What do family members search when a relative is arrested?

A family member looking for a defense lawyer often doesn't know the charge's legal name, so they search for the situation: the jail, the bail, the first court date. A defense firm needs pages written for that reader as well as for the accused.

Family searches use the words of someone standing outside the case. These are the patterns we plan family pages around:

[slot family]

A family page answers what the family can do today. It shows how to find the person in county custody, what the first appearance involves, how bail works there, and how to reach the firm.

It speaks to the caller, not the accused, and it never asks the family to describe the case in a web form.

When a family member calls, intake needs two answers a client's own call doesn't: who is calling, and who the lawyer would represent. Who hears what about the case is your firm's decision, so the page promises a callback, not a case discussion.

For a family, the first court date is the next deadline, and that date is where page priority starts.

## How do the first court dates change which defense pages matter?

The first court date sets the window in which a defense firm can win the call. In federal cases, [Rule 5 of the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcrmp/rule_5) requires taking an arrested person before a magistrate judge without unnecessary delay.

Each state sets its own first-appearance timing, so check your state's rules of criminal procedure. Whatever the number of hours or days, it is short, and the pages that matter in it must already rank.

We plan defense pages by the window each one serves:

[steps]
1. **Before the first appearance.** The Business Profile, the county page and the family page. There is no time to build them after the arrest.
2. **Before the plea or trial setting.** Charge pages and the fee page, read while the family compares firms.
3. **During the case.** Pages on the hearings in your court, for clients and families following along.
4. **After the case.** Record-clearing pages, found by people with no court date pressing.

Questions about a named court suit Google's AI answers: one place, one question, one clear answer. For a PI firm in New Orleans, the page for <q data-no-check>what to do after a truck accident in New Orleans</q> was cited 3 times in Google's AI answer. A first-appearance page for your county is built the same way.

The same window means some calls arrive when the office is closed.

## How should a criminal defense firm handle calls outside business hours?

A criminal defense firm should decide who answers outside business hours before its pages rank, and list only the hours it keeps. Google's [profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) ask for the firm's regular customer-facing hours.

This is the intake checklist we go through with a defense firm before new pages go live:

[checklist]
- **Name who answers after hours:** an on-call lawyer, an answering service, or a callback with a stated time.
- **Match the profile's hours to that answer,** and add holiday hours when the office closes.
- **Ask first whether the caller is the accused** or calling for someone else.
- **Take the county and the next court date** on every call, so the callback is ranked by urgency.
- **Send web forms to a phone,** not only to an inbox someone checks in the morning.
- **Log the page or search behind each call,** so each defense page is judged by the calls it brings.
- **Call your own line on a weekend evening** once a month and note what happens.

## Who decides whether a defense lawyer can say "specialist" or "board certified"?

Each US state's own rules decide whether a defense lawyer may claim to be a certified specialist. Most follow [ABA Model Rule 7.2(c)](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_2_advertising/), which allows the claim only with certification from an approved or ABA-accredited body named in the ad.

Outside the US, the law society or regulator sets the rule. The table shows how the wording changes by jurisdiction.

| Jurisdiction | Who sets the rule | What a defense page may say |
|---|---|---|
| US baseline | ABA Model Rule 7.2(c), adopted state by state | "Certified specialist" only with an approved certifier, named |
| Texas | [Rule 7.02(b)](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/advertisements) | "Board Certified", the area, then "Texas Board of Legal Specialization" |
| New York | Rule 7.1(c), since June 1, 2026 ([NYSBA](https://nysba.org/understanding-changes-to-new-york-state-lawyer-advertising-rules/)) | No certified-specialist claim without that certification |
| British Columbia | [Code rule 4.3-1](https://www.lawsociety.bc.ca/for-lawyers/act-rules-and-code/code-of-professional-conduct/chapter-4-%E2%80%93-marketing-of-legal-services/) | No "specialist" title unless authorized; a preferred field is allowed |
| Australia, most states | [ASCR rule 36.2](https://www.lawsociety.com.au/practising-law-in-NSW/ethics-and-compliance/regulatory-compliance/advertising-legal-services) | "Accredited specialist" only with the professional body's accreditation |
| England and Wales | [SRA Code 8.8](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-solicitors/) | Publicity must be accurate and not misleading |

## Defense practices that need a different plan

The page matrix above fits a defense practice in state court. Four kinds of work change it, and each needs its own pages: federal cases, juvenile and record clearing, non-citizen clients, and DUI.

### Federal charges

Federal cases run in the 94 US district courts ([uscourts.gov](https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/court-role-and-structure)), so a defense firm's federal page is built per district, not per county. Use the district's name as the court writes it, because that is the name on the court's own documents.

### Juvenile cases and record clearing

Record-clearing searches come from people with time to read and compare, not from a family in the first hours. Those pages can carry more detail, and they work all year rather than in the days after an arrest.

A juvenile page speaks to parents as well as to the young person charged, so it reads like a family page with the juvenile court's own steps.

### Clients who are not US citizens

A plea can change a non-citizen's immigration status. In [Padilla v. Kentucky](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/08-651) (March 31, 2010), the US Supreme Court held that counsel must tell a client whether a plea carries a risk of deportation.

A defense firm with non-citizen clients can say on its pages how it handles that question, for example by working with immigration counsel. Our page on [SEO for immigration lawyers](/seo-for-immigration-lawyers/) covers the immigration side of that same search.

### When DUI is a large share of the work

DUI cases bring their own searches, led by the license deadline that starts at the arrest. Our page on [SEO for DUI attorneys](/seo-for-dui-attorneys/) covers those pages, while the charge and county structure stays here. Each of these four lanes can still go wrong in the same ways.
