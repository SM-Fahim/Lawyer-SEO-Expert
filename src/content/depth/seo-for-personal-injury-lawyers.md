## Which pages does an injury firm need, by case type, city and claim stage?

An injury firm needs a page for every case type it wants and question pages for each stage of the claim. City versions come only where it has local facts to add.

Searches change as the claim moves: a nearby firm first, then deadlines, insurers and fees, then names and reviews. Here is the page map we plan from. Brackets mark a pattern, not a real search.

| Claim stage | What people type | Page that answers it | Build it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right after the crash | "[case type] lawyer near me" | Business Profile, then the case-type page | Always |
| The first days | <q data-no-check>what to do after a truck accident in New Orleans</q> | A "what to do after" page | You take that case type in that city |
| Looking for a firm by case | <q data-no-check>tanker truck accident lawyer in New Orleans</q> | Case-type page with the city | You take those cases there and know the local facts |
| A rare or serious crash | <q data-no-check>jackknife truck accident Louisiana</q> | Case-type page at state level | A city version would be thin |
| The deadline nears | "[state] [case type] filing deadline" | A deadline page that cites the statute | You practice in that state |
| Comparing fees | "how much does a [case type] lawyer charge" | One fee page for the whole firm | Always |
| Choosing between firms | "[firm name] reviews" | Profile reviews and lawyer bio pages | Always |

A rare case type gets a state page: it can have too few local searches for a city page.

### When does a city version earn its own page?

A city version of a case-type page earns its place when the firm takes those cases there and can say something only a local page can. Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) name city or region pages that all lead visitors to one destination as doorway abuse.

Before we write a city version, the plan names its local facts. Those are the court where the claims are filed, the roads or worksites behind the crashes, and how the firm meets clients there. No local facts, no city page.

If the current site can't hold this structure, our page on [lawyer web design built to rank](/lawyer-web-design-development/) covers how a rebuild keeps the pages that already rank. A map like this is a plan; the New Orleans truck pages show it working.

## Which injury searches get an AI answer, and who does it name?

In the New Orleans truck searches, Google's AI did two different things. For a search looking for a firm, it listed key firms by name; for a question, it cited the page that answered it.

So an injury firm needs both halves: a strong local presence for the firm-finding searches, and pages with the clearest answer for the questions.

As of November 2026, Google's guide to [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) asks for nothing special. A page has to be indexed and able to show with a snippet, and no extra markup or AI file is needed.

The same guide says AI Overviews appear only when Google's systems judge them additive to classic Search, so some injury searches show none. We run your main injury searches each month and note which firms and pages the AI names.

We write injury pages to be quoted, and each one follows four habits:

[checklist]
- **The answer comes first,** in plain words, in the opening lines.
- **The place is named,** because rules and deadlines change with it.
- **The source is cited:** a deadline page names its statute.
- **The date is shown,** so readers and machines know when it was checked.

## Who gets into the Map pack when an injury firm has more than one lawyer?

Google's [guidelines for representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) let each public-facing lawyer at a multi-lawyer firm have a Business Profile named with the lawyer's name only. The office keeps its own separate profile.

The <dfn>Map pack</dfn> is the short list of firms Google shows on a map above the regular results. Each eligible lawyer profile is one more listing that can appear there, if it follows the rules.

The same guidelines set three limits that catch injury firms out:

[checklist]
- **A solo lawyer at a branded firm shares one profile**, named "Firm: Lawyer".
- **No extra profiles per specialty.** A lawyer can't have one profile for truck cases and another for car cases.
- **Categories stay few.** Google asks for the fewest categories that describe the core business.

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking ([how local ranking works](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)). An injury firm can shape relevance with its services list and prominence with reviews and links. Distance is fixed by the office address.

Reviews are where injury firms slip. Google's [review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114) bars offering incentives for reviews and asking only happy clients for them.

In the US, the FTC rule in force since October 21, 2024 bans incentives tied to a positive review. It bans threats used to suppress a bad one too ([FTC questions and answers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)).

A client may mention a settlement amount in a review. If the firm then copies that review onto its own site, the firm is publishing a past result, and the settlement rules below apply.

## What may a PI site say about settlements, and how soon may a firm reach out?

The rules that most often change an injury site cover two things: how it reports past settlements, and how soon a firm may contact people after a crash. US lawyer advertising is regulated state by state, and most states start from the ABA Model Rules.

Under Model Rule 7.1, comment [3], even a true past result can mislead if it leads a reader to expect the same outcome ([ABA comment on Rule 7.1](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_1_communication_concerning_a_lawyer_s_services/comment_on_rule_7_1/)).

Under the ABA's comment on Rule 7.3, a website speaks to the public and is not solicitation; the timing rules bite on targeted texts, messages and mail. The table shows the injury rules that most often change a page or a campaign.

| Rule | Who sets it, where | What it means for an injury firm |
|---|---|---|
| Past results can mislead | ABA Model Rule 7.1, comment [3]; states adopt their own | Give settlement figures context; qualifying language can help |
| Reduced verdicts | [Texas Rule 7.01(g)](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/communications-concerning-a-lawyers-services) | An ad for a cut verdict must state what the client received |
| Air crash contact | [49 U.S.C. 1136(g)(2)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/49/1136), US federal law | No unsolicited contact with victims or relatives before day 45 |
| Rail crash contact | [49 U.S.C. 1139(g)(2)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/49/1139), US federal law | The same 45-day bar after fatal intercity rail crashes the NTSB investigates |
| Homepage filing | [Texas Rules 7.04](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/filing-requirements-for-advertisements-and-solicitation-communications) and [7.05(b)](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/communications-exempt-from-filing-requirements) | Homepage filed within 10 days unless exempt; other site pages are exempt |
| Waiting period dropped | New York, from June 1, 2026 ([NYSBA](https://nysba.org/understanding-changes-to-new-york-state-lawyer-advertising-rules/)) | The separate 30-day PI blackout is gone; live solicitation is still barred |

> **The New York row is new.** Advice written before June 2026 may still tell New York injury firms to wait 30 days, and that advice is now out of date.

Lawyer SEO Expert uses "expert" about search work only. It says nothing about a legal credential, and no page we draft calls your lawyers specialists unless your rules allow it and you approve. A lawyer at your firm, or your ethics counsel, signs off on each page before it is published.

## When the usual injury plan needs changing

The page map above fits a US injury firm with one main office, no bought leads and no backlog of thin pages. Three situations change it, each covered below.

### Injury solicitors and lawyers outside the US

Local rules outside the US change which injury pages are worth building. The table shows what changed in each market and what it means for the page plan.

| Market | What changed | What it means for the page plan |
|---|---|---|
| England and Wales | [Road claims under £5,000](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/making-a-personal-injury-whiplash-claim) use Official Injury Claim, from 31 May 2021 | Aim pages at higher-value and non-road claims |
| England and Wales | [Referral fees in injury claims](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/prohibition-of-referral-fees-in-laspo-56-60/) banned since 1 April 2013 | Search leads the firm earns itself carry more weight |
| England and Wales | [SRA Code 8.8 and 8.9](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-solicitors/): accurate publicity, no unsolicited approaches | Claims on every page must be provable |
| British Columbia | [Enhanced Care](https://icbc.com/claims/injury/if-you-want-to-take-legal-action) ended most crash lawsuits from May 1, 2021 | Pages on ICBC claim help and other injury types |
| Queensland | [PIPA s66](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/2026-10-29/act-2002-024) limits injury advertising, including "no win, no fee" claims | Website pages stay general information on negligence and terms |
| New South Wales | [Old injury advertising ban](https://www.lawsociety.com.au/practising-law-in-NSW/ethics-and-compliance/regulatory-compliance/advertising-legal-services) not carried into the Uniform Law | Normal page plan, under ASCR rule 36 on misleading ads |

The Official Injury Claim service is built to work without a solicitor, so low-value road pages are a weaker bet in England and Wales than serious injury and non-road claims. Check the current position with the SRA, your law society or the state regulator before a page goes live.

Firms outside the US can work with us remotely. Fahim's own record spans four countries, among them Idiya (New Zealand) and Breakthrough Maths (Ireland).

### Firms that buy leads today

Bought injury leads stop when the payments stop. Pages, a profile and reviews the firm owns keep working, and the ones we build belong to the firm.

In the US, ABA Model Rule 7.2(b) bars giving anything of value for recommending a lawyer, with a carve-out for the reasonable cost of permitted advertising ([ABA Rule 7.2](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_2_advertising/)). Each state adopts its own version, so ask your ethics counsel about any per-lead deal before you sign it.

### A site already full of thin accident pages

A site with dozens of near-identical accident pages is a risk, not an asset. Google's [spam policies on scaled content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) treat pages made in bulk to manipulate rankings, with little value to readers, as abuse.

The fix is to merge before writing more. We keep and improve the accident pages that bring calls and fold the rest into stronger case-type pages with redirects. The last section covers where agencies go wrong to leave a site this way.
