## What does a driver search at each stage of a DUI case?

A driver's DUI searches follow the case: a lawyer close by in the first hours, the license deadline in the first days, penalties before arraignment, then court logistics. A DUI firm wins more calls when a page is ready for each stage.

The timeline below is the one we plan DUI pages around. Searches in quotation marks are real searches drawn from keyword data; the rest are typical patterns.

| Stage | What the driver types | Page that answers it |
|---|---|---|
| First hours after release | "dui lawyer near me" | Business Profile and the main DUI page |
| The next morning | "what happens after a dui arrest in [state]" | A first-days page for your state |
| The license window | <q data-no-check>dmv lawyers</q> | A license-hearing page for each state |
| Before arraignment | <q data-no-check>should i get a lawyer for my first dui</q> | First-offense page |
| Court dates | <q data-no-check>can my lawyer go to court for me for dui</q> | A court-process page for DUI cases |
| A serious charge | <q data-no-check>felony dui attorney</q> | Felony and repeat-offense page |
| After the case | "[state] ignition interlock requirements" | Interlock and reinstatement page |

Two clocks run through that table. The court case moves at the court's pace, but the license window closes in days, so it needs a page before anything else.

## Why should each state's license deadline get its own DUI page?

Each state's license deadline deserves its own DUI page because it is short, exact and separate from the court case. California and Florida give drivers 10 days to ask for a hearing, and Texas gives 15.

| State | Days to ask for a hearing | Counted from | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| California | 10 | Receipt of the Order of Suspension/Revocation | [DMV Admin Per Se facts](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/aps-fact-sheet-2021-pdf) |
| Texas | 15 | Receiving, or being presumed to receive, the notice | [DPS Notice of Suspension](https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/Forms/DIC-25.pdf) |
| Florida | 10 | Issuance of the notice of suspension | [Florida Statutes 322.2615](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/322.2615) |

> **In California, most drivers let that window close.** The [DMV's own 2021 fact sheet](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/aps-fact-sheet-2021-pdf) counts 34,736 of 112,429 Admin Per Se actions challenged and scheduled for a hearing: less than a third. The rest went uncontested.

The hearing sits apart from the criminal case, too. Texas holds license hearings at its State Office of Administrative Hearings and treats them as [unrelated to criminal court proceedings](https://www.soah.texas.gov/representing-yourself-administrative-license-revocation-drivers-license-cases-state-office). Florida's statute says the license decision may not be considered at the DUI trial. That is why the license page and the criminal page answer different questions.

A deadline page is the kind of page Google's AI quotes. For <q data-no-check>Texas El Paso car accident filing deadline</q>, Google's AI answer cites a PI firm in El Paso first, through its deadline page. A license deadline question has that shape too: one number, one rule, one official source.

Google needs no special code for this, per its [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) page as of November 2026. The deadline page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet, nothing more. We check the AI answers for your deadline searches every month.

Deadlines and the way they are counted change by state and by notice. Each license page quotes its official source, shows the date it was checked, and gets checked against the notice drivers actually receive.

## Should DUI pages sit on the criminal defense site or a separate one?

DUI pages usually belong inside the firm's main defense site, in their own section, as long as every search has one clear owner page. A separate DUI site has to earn its links from zero.

Two pages on one site compete when they chase the same search. This split keeps DUI pages and criminal pages from doing that:

| Search | Owner page | Why that page |
|---|---|---|
| "dui lawyer [city]" | The main DUI page | DUI-only intent |
| "[state] dmv hearing after dui arrest" | The state's license page | An administrative case, not a criminal one |
| "criminal defense lawyer [county]" | The criminal county page | Covers every charge in that court |
| "[charge] lawyer [county]" for other charges | The criminal charge page | Not a DUI search |

Our page on [SEO for criminal defense lawyers](/seo-for-criminal-defense-lawyers/) covers charge and county pages. This page covers only what is DUI-specific.

The offense has other names in some states. Texas, for one, calls it DWI, the term its hearing office uses. Title each DUI page with the term your state uses, and add the other term only where drivers near you type it.

## What should a DUI firm's Google Business Profile list?

A DUI firm's Business Profile should list its real name, the fewest categories that describe the firm, a services list in DUI terms, and the hours someone answers. Google's [guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) bar added taglines in the name and categories used as keywords.

In practice that means a services list a driver recognizes: DUI defense, license hearings, refusal cases, under-21 cases and commercial driver cases. Each service should match a page on the site, so the profile and the pages tell Google the same story.

Hours matter for a DUI firm because the first searches come right after release. Google asks for the regular customer-facing hours, so a firm that answers late lists those hours and a firm that doesn't leaves them off.

Reviews come slowly in DUI work, since clients may not want the case known. Google's review policy bars reviews from staff and other conflicts of interest, as well as asking only happy clients ([review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114)). Every review has to come from a real client.

Who you compete with depends on the town: the Map pack for "dui lawyer" can go to DUI-only firms, general defense firms or firms from the next town. The ranking review shows which of them hold yours.

A new profile takes months, not days. For a PI firm in New Orleans, a second profile with no history logged about 1 call in July and 9 in October. A new DUI profile starts from zero the same way.

## What can a DUI firm say about won hearings and dismissed charges?

A won license hearing or a dismissed DUI is a past result, so each US state's version of ABA Model Rule 7.1 decides how it may appear. Texas, for example, calls a claim misleading if it is substantially likely to raise unjustified expectations about results.

Texas adds a rule DUI firms with more than one lawyer should know. Under comment 10 to [Texas Rule 7.01](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/communications-concerning-a-lawyers-services), a lawyer may claim credit for a prior result only after playing a substantial role in it.

Letters to recently arrested drivers are a separate channel with their own rules. [Texas Rule 7.03](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/solicitation-and-other-prohibited-communications) requires each solicitation to be marked "ADVERTISEMENT". Its comments place the word on the envelope and first page of a letter, or first in an email subject line.

Texas requires those letters to be filed with the State Bar within 10 days of being sent, under [Rule 7.04](https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/filing-requirements-for-advertisements-and-solicitation-communications).

## DUI work outside the US

Outside the US, the same offense goes by other names and other rules: drink driving in England and Wales, impaired driving in Canada. Both markets change what a DUI-style page must say.

### Drink driving solicitors in England and Wales

The SRA's [Transparency Rules](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/transparency-rules/) require a firm that advertises Magistrates' Court work on summary-only road traffic offences, dealt with at a single hearing, to publish prices for it. Check which of your offences fall in that group; for those, the page template changes.

The service page then shows the total cost or a range, the basis of the charges, who does the work, likely disbursements, VAT, and what the price includes.

The penalty facts must match GOV.UK. Its [drink driving penalties](https://www.gov.uk/drink-driving-penalties) page sets a ban of at least 1 year, or 3 years for a second conviction within 10 years.

### Impaired driving lawyers in Canada

In Canada, impaired driving is a Criminal Code offence. [Justice Canada](https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/cj-jp/sidl-rlcfa/index.html) lists a mandatory minimum fine for a first offence and mandatory jail for repeat offences.

Justice Canada notes that provinces and territories add their own laws. A Canadian page plan therefore pairs one federal penalty page with a page for each province's license rules.

Check the current rules with the SRA or your provincial law society before a page goes live. We take on drink driving and impaired driving firms abroad and work with them remotely; Fahim's own growth work includes Breakthrough Maths in Ireland and Idiya in New Zealand.

## Which DUI marketing shortcuts backfire?

DUI marketing shortcuts backfire when they trade accuracy for volume. These are the ones that cost DUI firms rankings or trust, each with the fix we use.

[checklist]
- **A page for every name of the offense.** DUI, DWI and OWI pages with the same text look like doorway pages. One page uses your state's term.
- **Deadline pages with no source or date.** A wrong number of days does more harm than no page. Each one cites its statute or form and shows the date checked.
- **Hours the firm doesn't keep.** A profile that promises late answers and sends calls to voicemail loses the caller. List the hours someone picks up.
- **Reviews from staff or friends.** They break Google's conflict-of-interest rule. Ask every client, and only clients.
- **DUI and criminal pages chasing the same search.** Two pages split one search between them. Each search gets one owner page.
- **Results as headlines.** A won hearing is still a past result, so it goes up only with approval and context.

SEO for DUI attorneys works when the license page, the main DUI page and the profile each answer their own search, and someone picks up when the driver calls.
